I build Android and Flutter apps that a large part of Uzbekistan uses every day.

Over 7+ years I have gone from developer to Android team lead: today I oversee our projects at Telecom Soft, guide how their architecture is built, mentor the team and still ship new products from zero. The applications I am responsible for carry more than 3.5M monthly active users in total.

What I have shipped:

• Joymee — property marketplace with 300K monthly active users, 4.8★ from 21,900 ratings; I work on the Android app.

• MyUzbekistan — travel companion for tourists across the country: both the Android and the iOS app built from scratch in Flutter.
• Mediabox (Android TV / OTT / VOD), TelecomCar, TelecomChat, TSoft HRM and a few smaller products.

How I work: clean architecture and MVVM, code that survives high load, security taken seriously — especially on apps that carry citizens' personal data — and a team that grows through review and mentoring.

Tech: Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose, Android SDK, Android TV, RxJava, Koin, Dagger 2 · Flutter, Dart, GetX, Dio, Hive · REST API, Retrofit, OkHttp, GraphQL, Firebase, Google Maps · Clean Architecture, MVVM, SOLID, Git, Jira.
